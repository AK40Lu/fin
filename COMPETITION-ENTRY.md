# Competition Entry — Fin Kids Business Coach

---

## The Build

Fin is a 1:1 business coach for kids, built with 12 markdown files in a Claude Project. No code. No app. No subscription.

The folder is the coaching system. CLAUDE.md is the orchestrator — it tells Claude who Fin is, what to load at the start of every session, how to handle the two people in the room (the kid and the parent), and what to say no to. Two files grow with the relationship: a kid profile and a venture log that Fin reads before saying a word in any session.

The rest is rules, identity, examples, reference frameworks, and a 6-week arc — all in plain text.

---

## The Thesis

1:1 tutoring outperforms classroom instruction by two standard deviations (Bloom, 1984). The average tutored student performs better than 98% of classroom-taught students. This is one of the most replicated findings in educational research — and it applies directly to entrepreneurship.

Most parents want their kids to learn business. Most parents don't have the domain knowledge to teach it. And no app, game, or curriculum replicates what a real coaching relationship produces: someone who knows your specific idea, tracks your specific progress, and tells you the specific truth about what's working and what isn't.

Fin is that relationship — built in a folder.

---

## What's New

**The idea is the curriculum.** Fin doesn't teach business concepts in the abstract. It teaches profit margin by calculating the exact margin on your kid's cookie business. It teaches customer discovery by asking who would actually buy the cookies, and why they'd choose them over the grocery store. Generic lessons are noise. Real numbers from a real idea stick.

**Two people per session.** Fin coaches the kid and the parent simultaneously, in different modes. The kid owns the business. The parent shows up as a co-learner — learning alongside, not above. At the end of every session, Fin gives the parent a specific co-learner brief: what to help with this week, and one specific thing not to do. This is not a babysitter in agent form. It's a coaching triangle that makes both participants better.

**The arc ends with a real sale.** The 6-week program ends with one real transaction — real money from a real customer who isn't family. Not a simulation. Not a pitch competition. A sale. Because the fastest way to learn what works is to try to sell something and hear what the market says.

## What Fin Says No To

- "Can you just write my business plan?" → No. You write it. I help you think.
- "I want to sell to everyone." → Name one person. Start there.
- "I'll start for real next week." → What's actually stopping you this week?
- Price set below cost → We fix this before you sell a single unit.
- Parent taking over the session → I want to hear [kid's name]'s take first.

## What Comes Next

The 6-week arc ends with a first sale. What happens after is up to the kid:

- **More sales** — scale the first idea, refine the pitch, grow the customer base
- **Scale** — redesign the operation to handle 10x volume
- **Next idea** — apply the arc to a second business, with the experience of the first

The pattern is replicable. The folder is the proof.
