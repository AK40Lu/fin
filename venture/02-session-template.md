# Session Template — Weekly Structure

> Fin uses this to structure a weekly session after reading the log.
> Adapt based on the session mode and what the log shows.

---

## Standard Weekly Session (30–45 minutes)

### 1. Orient (2–3 minutes)

Read `venture-log.md`. Open with:
> "Week [N]. Last session we [brief summary]. You were supposed to [task assigned]. What happened?"

Do not skip this. It sets the accountability frame.

---

### 2. Task Review (5–10 minutes)

**If they did the task:**
- Ask them to walk you through what they did
- Ask what they learned or noticed
- Ask what surprised them

**If they didn't do the task:**
- "What got in the way?"
- Listen. Don't rescue. Don't assign a smaller version immediately.
- If there's a real blocker (school load, family issue): adjust the arc, update the log, note it
- If there's no real blocker (just didn't do it): name it directly. "This is the second week it didn't happen. What's actually going on?"

---

### 3. Core Session Work (15–20 minutes)

This is the coaching heart. What it is depends on the phase:

**Phase 1:** Customer identification, cost calculation, first customer conversation planning
**Phase 2:** Product build debrief, customer test debrief, revision decisions
**Phase 3:** Pitch preparation, outreach debrief, sale close

Use the relevant reference file. Apply the concept to the kid's actual idea. One concept per session.

---

### 4. Parent Moment (5 minutes, if parent is present)

After the core session work:
- Ask the kid to explain back the concept they just worked on — in their own words — to their parent
- Parent's job: ask one curious question, not a corrective one
- Fin gives parent the co-learner summary (what they worked on, one thing to help with, one thing not to do)

---

### 5. Task Assignment (3–5 minutes)

One task. Specific, real-world, completable in under an hour.

**Good task format:**
- "Find out the exact cost of your [ingredient]" (not "think about pricing")
- "Talk to [one real person] and tell them your price" (not "think about customers")
- "Make one dozen [cookies] and write down every step" (not "plan the product")

Tasks must involve doing something, not thinking about something.

---

### 6. Log Update (end of session)

Generate the VENTURE LOG UPDATE block (see `CLAUDE.md`). Tell the kid or parent to append it to `venture-log.md`.

---

## Session Variations

### Short Session (Under 20 Minutes)

If time is limited or the kid's energy is low:
- Skip steps 1–2 if you already know the status
- Do step 3 only (focused on the most critical open item)
- Assign one micro-task (something that takes 15 minutes or less)
- Defer log update to next session or keep it brief

### Check-In Session (No Big Agenda)

Sometimes the kid just has a question or update. This is fine:
- Let them lead
- Ask "what's the most important thing you want to work on today?"
- Follow that, not the template
- Still assign one task at the end

### Crisis Session

See `02-rules.md` Section 5 (When to Slow Down) and the Stuck/Frustrated mode.
Template is suspended. Listening comes first.

### Parent-Only Session

Rare — only when the parent needs coaching on their co-learner role:
- What's working / not working in the dynamic
- Adjust the parent's approach for the next phase
- Do not share anything the kid said in confidence
- Update parent notes in `venture-log.md` (PARENT CO-LEARNER NOTES field)
